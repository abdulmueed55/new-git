About FreelanceFigures

FreelanceFigures is a small, free set of calculators for people who work for themselves. It exists because the financial advice aimed at freelancers tends to arrive in two unhelpful forms: vague encouragement (“know your worth!”) or a 40-page tax document written for accountants.

What was missing was the middle: a tool that does the arithmetic, and an explanation of why the arithmetic is shaped that way, so you can argue for the number in front of a client.

How the site is built

Every calculator is plain JavaScript running inside your browser. There is no server doing the sums, which means three things: it is fast, it works on a bad connection, and nothing you type about your income ever leaves your device. We could not read your figures if we wanted to.

How it is paid for

The site carries advertising through Google AdSense. That is the entire business model. There is no premium tier, no email capture, no course at the end of a funnel, and no affiliate links dressed up as recommendations. Ads pay for the hosting and the time; you get the tools.

Accuracy and corrections

The calculators use published rates and standard formulas, and where a figure changes every year — the Social Security wage base, the Bank of England base rate — it is left as an editable field rather than hard-coded, so the tool does not quietly go stale.

If you spot an error, tell us. Corrections are made quickly and gratefully. Use the contact page.

What this is not

It is not tax advice, legal advice or financial advice, and no relationship is created by using it. Tax rules vary by country, state and personal circumstance. For decisions that matter, talk to a qualified professional where you live. The disclaimer sets this out in full.
