Why these six

Most freelancers do not fail because the work is bad. They fail because of arithmetic: a rate set by copying somebody else, a tax bill nobody saved for, a project quoted at the optimistic estimate, and an invoice sitting unpaid for ninety days because chasing it felt rude.

Each calculator here deals with one of those failures, and each one explains the reasoning rather than just returning a number — because the number is only useful if you can defend it in front of a client or an accountant.

Honest limits

These are estimators. They use effective rates and sensible defaults rather than modelling every bracket, deduction, credit and local rule in every country. For filing a return, planning a restructure, or anything where being wrong is expensive, take the output to a qualified accountant in your own jurisdiction. See the full disclaimer.
