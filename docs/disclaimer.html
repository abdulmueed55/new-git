Estimates, not filings

Every figure produced on this site is an estimate. The calculators apply standard, published formulas to the numbers you type in. They do not know your deductions, credits, filing status quirks, local surcharges, treaty positions or business structure, and they do not attempt to model a full tax return.

Rates change

Tax rates, contribution ceilings, statutory interest rates and thresholds are revised regularly, often annually and occasionally mid-year. Where a figure is known to move — such as the Social Security wage base or the Bank of England base rate — it is presented as an editable field precisely so you can enter the current value. Check the current figure with the relevant authority before relying on a result.

Jurisdiction

Several calculators use United States rules, which are labelled as such. Others are deliberately country-neutral and ask you to supply your own rates. Nothing here is tailored to the law of any particular country, state or province.

No professional relationship

Using this site does not make us your accountant, tax adviser, lawyer or financial adviser, and no duty of care arises from your use of it. For filings, disputes, restructures, or any decision where being wrong carries a cost, consult a qualified professional licensed where you live.

Your responsibility

You remain solely responsible for the figures you enter, for how you interpret the outputs, and for every pricing, tax and business decision you make. If a result looks surprising, it is more likely that an input needs checking than that the surprise is correct — check it before acting.
