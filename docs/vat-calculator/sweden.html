Check the rate before you invoice. This field is prefilled with the figure commonly cited for Sweden, but rates move and the page you are reading may be older than the change. Confirm with Skatteverket and type the current figure in — the calculator works at any rate.

Registering in Sweden

Registration becomes compulsory at a low annual turnover limit, collected by Skatteverket.

Rates other than the standard one

12% on food and hotels, 6% on books, newspapers and transport.

Working backwards from a total

To find what you actually earned inside a figure that already includes the tax, divide rather than subtract. Taking a percentage off the gross always leaves too little, and the error grows with the rate. Switch the box above to the removing mode and the calculator does it correctly.

Whatever the rate, the money you collect was never income. It is held on behalf of Skatteverket and will be asked for. Freelancers who treat it as revenue are the ones who cannot pay at the end of the quarter — the set-aside calculator applies the same habit to income tax.
