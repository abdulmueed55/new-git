Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The short version

The numbers you type into the calculators never leave your browser. We do not ask you to register, we do not collect your name or email unless you choose to write to us, and we do not sell data. The site does use Google AdSense and Google Analytics, and those services set cookies — details below.

Calculator data

Every calculation on this site runs locally in JavaScript. Your income, expenses, invoice amounts and tax rates are processed on your own device and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers. When you close the tab, they are gone. The only thing stored on your device is your light or dark theme preference, kept in your browser's local storage.

Advertising cookies

This site is funded by advertising served through Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Analytics

Aggregate analytics may be used to understand which pages are read and which calculators are used. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Visitors in the European Economic Area and the UK

Where required, a consent banner asks for your permission before non-essential cookies, including advertising cookies, are set. You may withdraw consent at any time through your browser settings. Our lawful basis for processing is consent for advertising and analytics cookies, and legitimate interest for the basic security and delivery of the website.

Visitors in California

We do not sell personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act. You have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion; contact us to make such a request.

Children

This site is intended for adults running a business. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights and contact

To ask what data relates to you, or to ask for deletion, email hello@freelancefigures.com. Because the calculators store nothing, such requests are usually answered quickly and in the negative — there is rarely anything to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
