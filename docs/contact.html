Contact

Questions, corrections and suggestions are all welcome — particularly corrections.

Email

Write to hello@freelancefigures.com and you will normally get a reply within two working days.

Useful things to include

  • Reporting a wrong result? Send the page address and the exact numbers you entered, so the calculation can be reproduced.
  • Suggesting a calculator? Describe the decision you are trying to make, not just the tool — it usually leads to a better tool.
  • Advertising or partnership enquiries? Advertising is handled entirely through Google AdSense; direct placements and sponsored posts are not accepted.

What cannot be answered

Personal tax questions. Not because of unhelpfulness, but because answering them properly requires knowing your full circumstances and being licensed where you live — and answering them improperly could cost you money. For those, find an accountant or tax adviser in your own country.

We also cannot tell you what to charge a specific client. The calculators give you a floor based on your own costs and hours; what sits above that floor depends on the value of the result, how badly it is wanted and how few people could deliver it. Nobody can price that from the outside.

Corrections get priority

If a formula is wrong, a rate is out of date or an explanation is misleading, that is the most useful message you can send and it goes to the front of the queue. Include the page address and the figures you entered so the result can be reproduced exactly. Where a correction changes a number, the page is updated the same week and the change is noted on it.

Using the calculators in your own work

You are welcome to use any result in a client quote, a proposal or an invoice without asking and without attribution. The output belongs to you; it was produced from your figures on your machine. What is not permitted is republishing the written guides as your own, or embedding the calculators inside a paid product.
