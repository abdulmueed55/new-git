Guides

Everything the calculators assume you already know, explained properly.

A calculator gives you a number. It cannot tell you what to say when a client calls the number too high, when to walk away from work that pays well, or how to word the email that finally gets a ninety-day invoice paid. That is what these are for.

Everything here is written for people who are good at their craft and were never taught the business around it — which is nearly everyone who goes freelance. No motivational filler, no funnels, and specific numbers wherever a number makes the point better than an adjective.

Where a guide touches tax, it says which country the rule belongs to, because nothing is more useless than advice written for somewhere else. Where it touches a rate or a threshold that changes every year, it explains how the rule works rather than quoting a figure that will be wrong by the time you read it.

New guides are added most weeks. If something you need is missing, say so and it moves up the list.
